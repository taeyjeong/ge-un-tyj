Where part 2b fits

PADD (part 2a) covers only comprehensive agreements. CFR records women's share of negotiators for ceasefires, pre-negotiation and partial deals too, so it can test whether the type of agreement changes the picture.

Two datasets

Each dataset holds one half of the question

CFR tracker → the input

% of women among negotiators, mediators and signatories in 73 major peace processes, plus women in parliament. PA-X has no data on who was at the table.

PA-X → the outcome and controls

GeWom: does the agreement mention women, girls or gender? Plus stage and year, the two strongest predictors from part 1.

They're joined agreement to agreement: each CFR page names the agreement its negotiator figure describes, and that one agreement is found in PA-X by country and name. Giving every agreement in a process the same % would invent data. Colombia has 240 PA-X agreements, but CFR's 33% describes only the 2016 final deal.

Conclusions

The type of deal predicts gender provisions; women's share of negotiators doesn't, in either type

1 · No detectable link

Women were about 11% of negotiators whether or not the agreement mentioned women. The odds ratio stays near 1 with stage and year controlled.

2 · Stage still dominates

Comprehensive agreements mention women in 18 of 19 cases, matching part 1's main finding on a completely different sample.

3 · Women's share is low everywhere

Most agreements had under 15% women negotiators. There's little variation to compare, which makes an effect hard to see.

4 · Presence ≠ influence

Consistent with part 2a: there, women signatories mattered but women merely present didn't. CFR's negotiator share measures presence.

Limitations

What to keep in mind

  • Small sample. 33 agreements. "No evidence of an effect" is not "evidence of no effect"; the interval allows a moderate effect either way.
  • Selection. CFR covers 73 major processes chosen partly by data availability, mostly comprehensive deals. Results may not hold for local agreements.
  • Hand-collected data. Read from 73 pages, with URLs kept for checking. For 3 processes CFR's list and detail pages disagree; the detail page is used.
  • Mention, not substance. GeWom records whether gender is mentioned, not how strong the provision is.
